# 🏀 Predicting the NBA Rookie of the Year  

The **NBA Rookie of the Year** award has gained global prominence due to the increasing popularity of basketball and the substantial revenue from broadcasts and sponsorships. Rookies aim to showcase their best performances to secure the title and enhance their visibility in the league.  

This project analyzes the performance of **NBA rookies** compared to players from other basketball leagues, identifying the key characteristics necessary to win the award. Additionally, the study formalizes the **Rookie of the Year prediction** as a **classification problem**, leveraging machine learning techniques to predict the winner.  

## 📊 Methodology  
The analysis is based on **players' game statistics**, and the prediction process involves:  
- **Data Balancing**: Using **Synthetic Minority Oversampling Technique (SMOTE)** to address class imbalance.  
- **Machine Learning Models**: Implementing **k-nearest neighbors (KNN), logistic regression, and random forest** for prediction.  
- **Key Findings**:  
  - Players with a **balanced skill set**, **strong offensive potential**, and **higher playing time** have a greater chance of winning.  
  - Among the models, **k-nearest neighbors** demonstrated the best performance, achieving **99% accuracy and F1-score**.  

## 🚀 Technologies Used  
- **Python**  
- **Machine Learning**: Scikit-Learn, SMOTE  
- **Data Analysis**: Pandas, NumPy  
- **Visualization**: Matplotlib, Seaborn  

## 📌 Results & Insights  
The study provides valuable insights into what makes a successful **NBA Rookie of the Year**, offering a **data-driven approach** to identifying future winners.
